The Recommendation

What to build first: the call review workflow

Research pointed to one answer. The call review workflow was the center of gravity, the screen every contact center user touched daily, and struggled with most.

Leadership had pre-approved incremental fixes to the existing report. I recommended building a new console from the ground up instead, the higher-value path, and got leadership behind it before a pixel was designed.

Design Decision 01

Filter-first, not table-first

The legacy layout forced users into a table before they knew what they were looking for. Research showed their real mental model was a search: filter down first, then scan.

I led with filtering as the primary interaction and matched the pattern users already carried in.

Design Decision 02

An AI summary layer between list and transcript

There was no intermediate layer between the call list and the full transcript. I pushed for the AI summary to be the dominant content in the list, not tucked behind a click.

Customers didn't just want them, they wanted to download them, aggregate them across thousands of calls, and build workflows on top.

Design Decision 03 · Human–AI trust model

AI scores. Humans keep the override.

AI ran the first-pass evaluation and scoring. Reviewers kept override authority at every step. The scale of calls was impossible to have humans review. fully. The largest customers had millions of calls per month that were being scored in Invoca.

In a product where trust started at 50%, the model couldn't be a black box. Automation carried the volume, human judgment stayed in control, and the interface made both legible, verify in seconds instead of re-doing the work.

Reflection

The hardest part wasn't the design.

01
Earned research buy-in in phases

Structured research in short stages that each earned the next, instead of asking for trust upfront.

02
Navigated product–engineering tension

Engineering questioned whether this was the right investment at all. Kept it moving through leadership.

03
Introduced UX review mid-project

Sold a new quality checkpoint, design reviewing engineering work before QA, against real resistance.

"It's like code review, but for UX."

The framing from an engineering manager that finally made the UX review process click, it started here and carried into everything after.
